How to use a car loan calculator with extra payments

Auto loans are usually fixed-rate and level-payment. Adding even a small extra amount each month can reduce total interest and the number of payments.

Steps

  1. Enter the vehicle loan amount you were quoted—not a sample rate from the internet.
  2. Type the annual percentage rate from your finance offer.
  3. Set the term (often 36–72 months).
  4. In Extra monthly payment, enter an amount you could add consistently.
  5. Review Saved with extra payments, the chart, and the amortization table.
  6. Export CSV or Print / save PDF for your records.

Tips

  • Confirm whether your lender applies extras to principal without penalties.
  • One-time fees can be added in the optional fees field for a rough total cost.
