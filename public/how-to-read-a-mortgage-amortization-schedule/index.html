How to read a mortgage amortization schedule

An amortization schedule shows how each payment splits between interest and principal until the balance reaches zero.

Columns

  • No. — payment sequence.
  • Date — due date based on your start date (first payment one month later).
  • Payment — regular installment (level payment loans).
  • Extra — optional additional principal you entered.
  • Principal — portion that reduces the balance.
  • Interest — charge on the remaining balance for that period.
  • Balance — amount still owed after the payment.

Mortgage planning

Enter your own home loan amount and rate. For long terms (e.g. 30 years), early rows show more interest than principal; later rows flip. Display labels such as USD or RWF do not change the math.

Disclaimer

Real mortgages may include escrow, insurance, PMI, and different day-count conventions. Use this schedule for planning; verify with your servicer.
