About Free Loan Calculator

Free Loan Calculator is a browser-based tool on web-free.site. You enter loan amount, annual interest rate, and term; the page computes monthly payment, total interest, payoff date, and a full amortization schedule with optional extra payments.

Upstream project

Calculation logic is derived from TekiDeki/Loan-Calculator by Kristian Mario Mišković, licensed under the Apache License 2.0. A copy of the license is in the repository LICENSE file. We thank the upstream author for publishing this work.

What we changed

  • Rebranded for web-free.site with English-only content and additional guides.
  • Currency display labels (RWF, CDF, FCFA, EUR, USD) without FX APIs.
  • CSV and print/PDF export of the schedule.
  • No sample bank rates—you supply every figure.
