Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a loan payment?

Enter principal, annual rate (%), and term. The tool uses standard fixed-rate level payments.

Are currency labels converted?

No. RWF, CDF, FCFA, EUR, and USD labels only change how amounts are formatted—not exchange rates.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser.
