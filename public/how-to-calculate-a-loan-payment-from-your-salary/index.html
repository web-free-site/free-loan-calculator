How to calculate a loan payment from your salary

Lenders look at income, existing debt, and policy limits. This guide helps you plan a monthly payment using your own salary and expense figures—not preset bank rates.

Steps

  1. Know your net monthly income. Use take-home pay after tax and mandatory deductions.
  2. List fixed costs. Rent, utilities, transport, insurance, and minimum debt payments.
  3. Set a payment ceiling. Many households aim to keep total debt payments below a portion of net income; your comfort level may differ.
  4. Enter your loan in the calculator. Type the amount you might borrow, the annual interest rate quoted to you, and the term in years or months.
  5. Compare the monthly payment to your ceiling. Adjust amount or term until the payment fits your budget.
